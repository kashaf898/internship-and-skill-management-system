## Project Overview

The Internship and Skill Management System is a proposed platform that helps students find internships, manage their skills, and track their applications. Companies can post internship opportunities and review applications, while administrators manage users and internship listings.

## Project Objectives

- Help students find relevant internship opportunities.
- Allow students to manage their profiles and skills.
- Enable students to apply for internships and track application status.
- Allow companies to publish internship opportunities and review applications.
- Provide basic skill-gap recommendations to help students identify skills they may need to improve.
